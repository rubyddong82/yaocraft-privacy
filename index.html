Yaocraft Privacy Policy

Applies to the Yaocraft Android app (package com.cubeworld.mobile). Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Yaocraft is a block-building game with a single-player mode and an online multiplayer mode. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: the app has no ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking services, and does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Information stored on your device

  • Worlds and settings. Single-player worlds, your player name, view distance, touch sensitivity and similar settings are stored locally in the app's private storage. They are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Single-player mode runs entirely on your device and works without sending your world anywhere.

Information sent when you play multiplayer

If you join an online server, the app connects over the internet to the game server operated by the developer and sends:

  • the player name you choose (up to 16 characters; do not use your real name or personal information);
  • your in-game position, movement and block changes so other players can see them;
  • chat messages you type, which are visible to other players on the server.

Like any internet connection, the server necessarily receives your IP address. The server's operational logs may record player names, chat messages and connection events for moderation, troubleshooting and keeping the service running. Multiplayer traffic is not end-to-end encrypted, so do not share passwords or personal information in chat or in your player name.

Optional administrator sign-in

The app contains a "Play as admin" option intended for the developer and approved administrators. If you use it, the app sends the email address you enter to the developer's sign-in service (rubyddong82.com), which emails you a one-time code. After you enter the code, a sign-in cookie is stored on your device and sent to the game server to confirm your administrator status. Regular players never need to use this feature.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics providers. The app contains no advertising or analytics code. Chat messages and in-game activity are, by their nature, visible to other players on the same multiplayer server.

Other network requests

If you tap the map-data credit link in the app, your browser opens the OpenStreetMap copyright page. That page is operated by OpenStreetMap and is governed by its own privacy policy. Real-city maps used for new single-player worlds are bundled inside the app.

Children

Yaocraft is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because multiplayer includes player-to-player chat, parents should supervise younger players. To request removal of a child's player name or chat content, contact us using the details below.

Retention and your choices

  • You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • You can avoid sending any data to the developer by using single-player mode only.
  • To ask us to remove your player name or chat messages from multiplayer server records, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact
